# Staff Enablement

The project included a structured training component for clinic staff.

## Training objectives

The training was designed to help staff:

- operate the automated workflow;
- understand the digital survey process;
- manage message templates and follow-up flows;
- troubleshoot common issues;
- maintain the system;
- adapt the workflow for future operational needs.

## Topics

The project documentation describes training across areas including:

1. digitalisation tools for survey and process capture;
2. information-processing and workflow automation;
3. RPA concepts and related automation tools.

## Operational handover

The admin/support guides provide practical information on:

- system components;
- sheet structure;
- configuration;
- deployment;
- daily messaging operations;
- batch execution;
- status refresh;
- logs;
- triggers;
- error scenarios;
- recovery procedures.

## Why this matters

The objective was not only to automate a process but also to leave the operating team with enough capability to sustain and improve it.

That makes user enablement and support design part of the solution architecture rather than an afterthought.
